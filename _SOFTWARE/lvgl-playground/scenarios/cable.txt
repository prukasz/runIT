# (SYS_DISPLAY_FLAG_READABLE): the adapter reads HEALTH every second. Off: nothing answers (MISO reads 0xFF),
# ERR_DISPLAY_LOST once. On: the panel powered on asleep with noise in its memory, HEALTH RESTARTED, the adapter
# resets the display and redraws the screen.
# Frames: [key][dir 00 read / 01 write][...], little-endian. Device ids here: the display 1, the touch 2, the GPIO 3.
