6 · Variables (interactive)

The engine's tree. Search opens folders. A reference shows the variable it points to, or Choose… while unset. The tree the engine refuses shows what checkVariables returns, under the tiles it is about.

7 · Devices

The project's devices (left) and the board's catalog (right), both from the descriptors. A device has an id and a name; a system device is the board's own. Pins are named by the descriptor (CH0, AIN3, Pin 22).

8 · Blocks

A block's pins are what a load or run error points at (programRefs). Enable pins read Run when, the enable output When done.

9 · Error log

Records a board sent, read by ErrorInterpreter. Click an entry to mark what it points at in the lists above: the device, the pin, the block pin, the variable.

10 · Help mode

The toolbar's Help button outlines each element that has help: a form row from its note, a button from its title. A click on an outline shows its text beside it; a click elsewhere or Escape hides it.
